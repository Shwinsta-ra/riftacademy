## Thread/topic: staging-feedback-round4

**Sections likely affected:** 3, 6, 9

**Customer-facing:**
Fixed a real answer-leak bug: "What's this card's Power cost?" questions were covering the wrong part of the card, leaving the actual power-cost pips fully visible. The might mask is also now exactly the same size as the energy-cost mask (previously a bit bigger), and two tutorial lines were tightened up ("You're ready. **Team-facing:**
Four items from Ashwin's fourth round of staging feedback, in `fix/staging-feedback-round4` (branched off latest `integration`).

1. **Real answer-leak fixed: power-cost questions were masking the energy pip, not the power pips.** `POSITION_MODE_KEY` in `attributeQuiz.ts` previously mapped both `energyCost` and `powerCost` to the same `"cost"` region (the top-left energy badge) on the theory that they're "two testable stats, not two visual spots." That assumption was wrong: power cost is printed as its own separate element, a stack of 1-4 small pip icons in a rounded capsule directly below the energy badge, not a number sharing the energy badge's spot. Confirmed by downloading and pixel-measuring several real cards (a Spell, a Unit, and two Gear cards of different rarities) -- the capsule's pip count visibly scales with `card.power` (1 pip for power=1, 3 pips for power=3, etc.), and the energy badge stays fully visible and unmasked while the actual pip stack (the real answer) was exposed the whole time a `powerCost` question was on screen. Fixed by giving `powerCost` its own dynamically-sized mask region: `getMaskRegions` now computes the capsule's height as `heightIntercept + heightPerPip * card.power` (both constants measured off real card art, per card type -- default vs Gear have different left-offsets and pip spacing since Gear's diamond-shaped energy badge sits differently than the default circular one), rather than looking up a static rect. `quizPositions.json` gained a new `"power"` section holding those per-type constants. Live-verified: a Power-cost question on a Spell card now masks the pip stack directly below the energy pip, leaving the energy value itself correctly visible.
2. **Might mask resized to exactly match the cost mask.** Per direct comparison feedback ("still slightly bigger... cost is the perfect size"), `might.default` in `quizPositions.json` now uses the identical width/height as `cost.default`, repositioned to stay centered on the printed might digit (verified against both a plain Unit's and a wider Champion-template digit, with margin to spare on the champion case). `might.Gear` was deliberately NOT shrunk to match: equipment's "+N" bonus text measures up to ~98px wide on some cards (verified against a second Epic-rarity gear card), which cost's circle size can't safely cover without risking a leak, so it keeps its own larger, safe size from the previous round.
3. **Shortened the final tutorial step's copy** from "You're ready! Tap here to start studying. RiftRecall will bring back anything you miss." to "You're ready. Tap here to start studying!"
4. **First tutorial step now welcomes to the app, not the feature**: "Welcome to RiftRecall!" changed to "Welcome to RiftAcademy!" (the tutorial's own opening line, independent of the separate first-launch Welcome screen from the previous round -- this line still runs on its own whenever the tutorial starts, including via "Replay tutorial").

**New standing rule or convention worth capturing:**
None new -- item 1 confirms the existing lesson (verify masks against real card pixels, don't assume a shared visual spot for two similarly-named stats) applies just as much to deciding whether two modes share a region in the first place, not just to sizing the region once you know they do.

**Anything another thread working today should know before touching related code:**
`getMaskRegions` in `attributeQuiz.ts` no longer treats every mode as a static-rect lookup -- `powerCost` is special-cased with a per-card formula (see `PowerRegion`/`heightIntercept`/`heightPerPip`). If a future mode's visual footprint also scales with the card's own data (not just its type), follow that same pattern rather than trying to force it into the static `RawRegion[]` table.
